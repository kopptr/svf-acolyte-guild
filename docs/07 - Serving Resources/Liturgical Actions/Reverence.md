An action made to show reverence, typically to the Eucharist itself, an [[Altar]] of Sacrifice, or a relic. In terms of which actions are used to reverence what items in what situations, we follow the #dominican customs.

- [[Major Inclination]]
- [[Moderate Inclination]]
- [[Minor Inclination]]
- [[Genuflection]]

An inclination (bow) is the most common form of reverence. In the [[Dominican Rite]], there are three types: [[Minor Inclination]], [[Moderate Inclination]], and [[Major Inclination]]. The [[Roman Rite]] only has the first two, known there as the [[Minor Inclination|Simple Bow]] and [[Moderate Inclination|Moderate Bow]].
